WE INCREASE YOUR PROBABILITIES OF SUCCESS

Our working system allows you to get direct quotations from the factories we are dealing with. You will be able to compare prices, delivery conditions and proper payment terms. All this in a granted minimum time as we are using the new technologies for the control and management of each Project.

Worldfast simplifies the procedures:

• Signature of purchase contracts with factories
• Payment surveyance
• Ensure delivery Schedule
• Provide pictures, packing, pallets, containers
• B/L notification, Packing List, Invoices, Quality Certificates and Certificates of Origin.

HOW DO WE WORK

Product and supplier research according to customer’s needs.

Negotiation with the suppliers the best price options, best deliveries and proper payment terms.

Complete mass production control.

Quality control before packing (Dimensional control, Mechanical properties and Chemical composition).

Logistical assessment and delivery conditions (FOB, CIF, FCL, LCL).

Follow up of shipment and delivery procedures as well as clearance documents for import purposes.

ABOUT US

More than 30 years experience in fasteners mainly from Asia. We only work with factories under previous visit and being certified by us.

Therefore, we feel prepared to provide any quotation covering the whole range of fasteners and other products related to.

- Quality assurance
