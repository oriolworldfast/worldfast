WHAT IS CBAM

CBAM (the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism, Regulation (EU) 2023/956) puts a price on the CO₂ emissions of certain goods imported into the EU. Screws, bolts, nuts, washers and similar fasteners are included: they fall under CN code 7318 (the Combined Nomenclature code that EU customs use to classify these products).

The European importer pays it by buying CBAM certificates for the emissions contained in each tonne. In practice, this cost is passed on to the price the importer is willing to pay its supplier. That is why it has a direct effect on the factory's competitiveness.

Embedded emissions
The CO₂ emitted to make the product: from the plant itself (heat-treatment furnaces, boilers, dryers, etc.) plus from the steel it is made of.
Default value
The emissions figure set by the European Commission for each country and product. It applies when the factory does not prove its real emissions.
Monitoring plan
The document that describes how the factory measures and calculates its emissions, with the content required by EU rules.
Accredited verifier
An independent body, accredited for CBAM, that reviews the plan and the data and issues the verification report.

THE PROBLEM: DEFAULT VALUES

If the factory cannot prove its real emissions, the importer has to declare the default value. It is deliberately high and it carries a mark-up that grows every year:

AN EXAMPLE WITH OFFICIAL FIGURES

A steel screw manufacturer in China, exporting 5,000 tonnes a year to the EU, with CBAM certificates at €75 per tonne of CO₂. The EU benchmark deduction is the part of the emissions for which no certificates have to be bought (it reflects the free allocation that European producers receive). It is calculated differently depending on whether default values or real data are declared: with real data, the actual production route of the steel mill is taken into account, and in this example the deduction is larger.

THE SOLUTION: A VERIFIED MONITORING PLAN

To declare real emissions you need a monitoring plan that complies with the rules, with emissions calculated using the official EU method and checked by an accredited verifier. That is what our platform, cbam.worldfast.es, produces. It was built exclusively for CBAM applied to fasteners.

  • Brings all plant data together: installation, production processes, emission sources, fuel consumption, raw material (steel wire rod and bars), suppliers, stock and production by CN code.
  • Calculates embedded emissions for each CN code with the official methodology for the definitive period (Regulation (EU) 2023/956 and Implementing Regulations (EU) 2025/2547, 2025/2620 and 2025/2621, as corrected by 2026/1740). Where a steel supplier provides no verified data, it applies the official default values.
  • Produces the monitoring plan (Monitoring Methodology Documentation) in English, with the content required by Annex II of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2547, ready for the verifier. Every version is recorded.
  • Calculates the EU benchmark deduction and the number of CBAM certificates for each tonne exported.
  • Prepares the reports for your importers, so they can declare real emissions instead of default values.
  • Stays up to date when the rules or official values change: default values, benchmarks, CBAM factor and certificate prices.

The whole service is delivered online, with no plant visits and no travel costs.
