WHAT IS CBAM?

Your EU customer pays for the carbon embedded in your screws. The amount depends on your data.

The EU importer must surrender CBAM certificates based on the emissions embedded in the fasteners it imports. The emissions can be determined using EU default values or verified actual values.

Without a monitoring plan

Default value

The European Commission assigns your country and product a deliberately high value, with a mark-up that rises every year (+10 % in 2026, +20 % in 2027, +30 % from 2028).

Steel screw, China, 2026: 7,01 t CO₂e/t

With a verified monitoring plan

Actual emissions

What your plant really emits: the steel you buy, your power, your material yield. Calculated using the official EU methodology and confirmed by an accredited verifier.

Example: 2,07 t CO₂e/t

THE MONITORING PLAN

The monitoring plan is the document that makes it possible

The monitoring plan is the basis for your CBAM emissions data. It defines how your plant identifies, measures and records the information needed to calculate the embedded emissions of your products. It covers your production processes, energy and fuel consumption, purchased steel and other relevant precursors, production volumes, material flows and calculation methods. Without a robust monitoring plan, your actual emissions cannot be consistently calculated, documented and prepared for verification.

Monitoring plan

Describes how emissions are measured and calculated.

Production data

Steel, energy, production and material yield.

Verification

An accredited verifier confirms the emissions data.

CBAM Registry

Verified emissions data available to your European customers.

THE TIMELINE

  • 2026First year subject to CBAM. All 2026 data must be reported.
  • Jan–Feb 2027Verification of 2026 data begins; certificates on sale from February.
  • 30 Sept 2027Deadline for the 2026 declaration.
  • 2027–28Default-value mark-up: 20 % in 2027 and 30 % from 2028.
  • 2029–34The benchmark deduction is phased out: it reaches zero in 2034.

Reliable emissions data can become a competitive advantage. Manufacturers with robust, traceable and verifiable emissions data can provide their European customers with greater confidence and reduce reliance on EU default values. Preparing your emissions data now puts your company in a stronger position for the CBAM verification and reporting process.
